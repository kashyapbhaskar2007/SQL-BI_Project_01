# Project Summary – Olist E-commerce Analysis

## Objective
Analyze the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset using SQL to extract useful business insights on sales, delivery, payments, and customer satisfaction.

## Key Findings

- **Top Categories:** Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, and Bed & Bath generated the highest revenue.
- **Delivery Performance:** A notable share of orders were delivered late, and late deliveries clearly reduced customer review scores.
- **Payments:** Credit Card is the most used payment method, often with installments.
- **Geography:** Both customers and sellers are highly concentrated in São Paulo (SP).
- **Freight Cost:** Some categories have high shipping costs relative to product price, affecting margins.
- **Sales Trend:** Overall monthly sales and revenue showed growth over time.

## Recommendations
- Improve logistics performance to reduce late deliveries.
- Reduce late deliveries to improve customer satisfaction.
- Focus on high-revenue categories.
- Optimize shipping for high-freight products.

## Conclusion
The analysis successfully converted raw data into clear actionable business insights using SQL queries and views.
